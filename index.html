👩 Big Sister
Happy Birthday to my youngest sister, Xiao Ting ❤️

In every family, there is always that one person who brings unexpected laughter into the room. For us, that person has always been you.

You have always been the most outstandingly funny person in our family — not because you were trying to be funny, but because all your silly little actions, random ideas, and “I know what I'm doing” confidence somehow became part of the joy you brought into our home. 😂

You have always been the special one: the rebellious one who didn't really like to follow my instructions; the daring one who was never afraid to try something new; the shining one with those big, bright eyes that always carried so much hope; and of course, the pretty one since young, with those sweet facial features and the smile that everyone remembers.

Growing up with you was not always easy for me 😂 There were times you didn't finish your homework, and somehow it became my responsibility — you really gave me quite a few headaches back then.

But looking back now, I realise that through you, I also learned a lot. You taught me that not everyone sees life the same way. You taught me that generosity can come so naturally. You taught me that love can be expressed in simple, unexpected ways.

That is the beauty of you. You have your own way of seeing the world.

Today, I am really happy to see you in this stage of life with someone who truly loves you. Someone who connects with you through the things you enjoy, cooks for you, takes care of you, and showers you with all the love you deserve.

I am also very happy that you finally found something that keeps your heart going: pet grooming. Congratulations on finding a career that you are passionate about! 🥳

May this new year of your life bring you more courage, more growth, more love, and more reasons to shine brightly as ever.

Happy Birthday, Xiao Ting. Continue to be the light, laughter, and love that you are. ❤️

With Much Love,
Xiao Yen.

👦 Your Big Bro
Happy 35th Birthday, Daphne!!

Thirty-five years ago, our family got its 开心果 (Happy nut).

I mean that with all the love in the world. You were the one who never stopped smiling, never stopped moving, never — not once — stopped saying yes to whatever challenges came next. And thirty-five years later, watching you, I don't think that's changed one bit.

You were the entertainment. Free of charge. You'd strike a pose nobody asked for. And somehow, every single time, you'd get the whole family laughing, telling you how ridiculous you were.

You loved being the reason the room got louder. And honestly? So did we.

You fell. And you found your feet — again and again. You found your way to pet grooming — and it turned out the same hands that once drew imaginary worlds, the same hands that once hugged every stray creature that crossed your path, were exactly the hands that job needed.

You live. You love. You explore. You have fun. And you have no regrets.

This has been your adventure — bitter and sweet, fearless and tender, changing shape as many times as it needed to. And I've been lucky enough to watch every version of it.

Happy 35th birthday, Daphne. Here's to whatever adventure comes next — I'll be cheering you on, same as always. ❤️

Love,
Your big brother
